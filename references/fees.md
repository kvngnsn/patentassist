# USPTO utility patent fees

All amounts are US dollars. Columns are **large** (undiscounted), **small** (37 CFR 1.27) and **micro** (37 CFR 1.29). Fee codes are listed as large/small/micro.

## Which fee rule is in force

- The fee rule in force is **"Setting and Adjusting Patent Fees During Fiscal Year 2025"**, 89 FR 91898 (Nov 20, 2024). It took effect on **January 19, 2025**.
- The USPTO fee schedule page says "Effective January 19, 2025 (Last revised November 1, 2026)".
- The **Nov 1, 2026** revision changed only one fee: a PCT search fee paid to a foreign office (Korea MOIP as searching authority, $783 to $867). No US filing, exam, issue or maintenance fee changed in 2026.
- **Aug 13, 2026:** the higher petition fee in 1.17(m)(1) now applies when a petition is filed **more than 1 year** after the missed action. Before this date the line was 2 years. The amounts did not change (91 FR 37826).
- **No 2026 proposed patent fee rule was found** in the Federal Register (searched for USPTO rules and proposed rules from Oct 2024 to Nov 2026).
- The USPTO's AIA section 10 fee-setting authority was due to lapse in mid-September 2026. Press reports say a continuing resolution extended it to about Dec 11 or 12, 2026 *(unconfirmed: secondary sources only)*. A lapse would stop new fee rules. It would not change the fees already in force *(unconfirmed: inference)*.
- Discounts are set by statute: small entity 60% off, micro entity 80% off (35 U.S.C. 41(h)(1); AIA section 10(b) as amended by the Unleashing American Innovators Act of 2022).

## Surcharges and add-on fees at filing

| Fee | 37 CFR | Codes | Large | Small | Micro |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Non-DOCX surcharge (spec, claims or abstract not in DOCX) | 1.16(u) | 1054/2054/3054 | 430 | 172 | 86 |
| Non-electronic (paper) filing fee, utility | 1.16(t) | 1090/2090/3090 | 400 | 200 | 200 |
| Each independent claim over 3 | 1.16(h) | 1201/2201/3201 | 600 | 240 | 120 |
| Each claim over 20 (any type) | 1.16(i) | 1202/2202/3202 | 200 | 80 | 40 |

| Utility size fee, per 50 sheets (or fraction) over 100 | 1.16(s) | 1081/2081/3081 | 450 | 180 | 90 |
| Surcharge: late filing, search or exam fee, late inventor's oath or declaration, no claim on filing, or filing by reference | 1.16(f) | 1051/2051/3051 | 170 | 68 | 34 |
| Non-English specification: translation processing fee | 1.17(i)(1) | 1053/2053/3053 | 150 | 60 | 30 |

Notes:

- **Non-DOCX surcharge.** It applies to original utility applications filed on or after Jan 17, 2024 under 35 U.S.C. 111. It does not apply to design, plant or provisional applications (1.16(u)). Continuations, divisionals and CIPs are covered. A preliminary amendment filed on the same day is part of the original disclosure, so it must also be DOCX to avoid the surcharge. Drawings may be PDF with no surcharge. Source: uspto.gov/patents/docx FAQ.
- **Paper filing fee.** Micro entities pay the small entity amount ($200). There is no micro discount (micro entity status page).
- **Late declaration surcharge.** The 1.16(f) surcharge is due if a signed declaration for every inventor is not present on the filing date. This is true even when the declaration is lawfully postponed under 1.53(f)(3). The surcharge must be paid within the Notice to File Missing Parts period. It cannot wait until allowance (MPEP 601.01(a)).
- **Counting claims.** A multiple dependent claim counts as the number of claims it refers to. Any claim that depends from a multiple dependent claim counts the same way (1.75(c)). Excess claim fees are due on filing or "later presentation at any other time" (1.16(h), (i)).
- **Size fee counting.** The size fee counts the specification (with claims and abstract) plus drawings. For e-filed applications, the page count is **75% of the sheets as rendered by Patent Center** (1.52(f)(2); MPEP 607). Sequence-listing and computer-program-listing text or XML files are excluded. A preliminary amendment present on the filing date is counted. How USPTO rounds the 75% figure is *(unconfirmed)*.

## Continuing-application fees (new in the Jan 19, 2025 rule)

These fees are due when a nonprovisional application claims benefit under 35 U.S.C. 120, 121, 365(c) or 386(c) and is filed long after the earliest benefit date (1.17(w)).

| Fee | 37 CFR | Codes | Large | Small | Micro |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Filed more than 6 and no more than 9 years after the earliest benefit date | 1.17(w)(1) | 1055/2055/3055 | 2,700 | 1,080 | 540 |
| Filed more than 9 years after the earliest benefit date (less any (w)(1) already paid) | 1.17(w)(2) | 1056/2056/3056 | 4,000 | 1,600 | 800 |

A claim to a **provisional** under 35 U.S.C. 119(e) does not trigger this fee. The rule lists only 120, 121, 365(c) and 386(c).

## Information disclosure statement (IDS) fees

| Fee | 37 CFR | Codes | Large | Small | Micro |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IDS fee (when 1.97(c) or (d) requires it) | 1.17(p) | 1806/2806/3806 | 280 | 112 | 56 |
| IDS size fee: cumulative items exceed 50 (up to 100) | 1.17(v)(1) | 1832/2832/3832 | 200 | 200 | 200 |
| IDS size fee: cumulative items exceed 100 (up to 200), less amounts already paid | 1.17(v)(2) | 1833/2833/3833 | 500 | 500 | 500 |
| IDS size fee: cumulative items exceed 200, less amounts already paid | 1.17(v)(3) | 1834/2834/3834 | 800 | 800 | 800 |

- **No entity discount** applies to the IDS size fees. The schedule lists the same amount in all three columns.
- **What counts.** Every item listed under 1.98(a)(1) counts, including duplicates. The count runs for the life of one application. It does not reset after an RCE. It starts at zero in a continuation, CPA or reissue. Items cited only in a parent are not counted unless they are listed again (89 FR 91898 at 91924).
- **Written assertion.** Every IDS must include a clear written assertion that the right size fee is enclosed, or that none is due (1.98(a)(4)). Form PTO/SB/08c is the stand-alone assertion. The Patent Center auto-load SB/08 (rev. 03-26) has the assertion built in.
- **When the statement or fee is due** (37 CFR 1.97):

| When the IDS is filed | What must come with it |
|---|---|
| (b) Within 3 months of filing, or before the first Office action on the merits, or before the first action after an RCE (whichever is later) | Nothing extra (only any size fee) |
| (c) After (b), but before a final action, notice of allowance, or other action closing prosecution | 1.97(e) statement **or** the 1.17(p) fee |
| (d) After (c), on or before paying the issue fee | 1.97(e) statement **and** the 1.17(p) fee |

No extension of time applies to IDS windows (1.97(f)). Details are in forms-and-filing.md.

## Track One prioritized examination

| Fee | 37 CFR | Codes | Large | Small | Micro |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Request for prioritized examination | 1.17(c) | 1817/2817/3817 | 4,515 | 1,806 | 903 |
| Processing fee (required with the request) | 1.17(i)(1) | 1830/2830/3830 | 150 | 60 | 30 |
| Publication fee (required if not already paid) | 1.18(d)(1) | n/a | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| **Track One add-on total** | | | **4,665** | **1,866** | **933** |

Rules from 37 CFR 1.102(e):

- **Claim limits:** no more than 4 independent claims, no more than 30 total claims, and no multiple dependent claim.
- **Fixing claims:** excess claims may be cancelled within 1 month of the first decision on the request. That month cannot be extended.
- **Must be complete on filing.** The request must be filed with an original utility or plant application. A utility application must be filed electronically. It must include the filing, search and exam fees and at least one claim. It must also include the inventor's oath or declaration, or an ADS that lets the declaration be postponed under 1.53(f)(3).
- **After an RCE:** one Track One request may also be granted with or after an RCE. It must be filed before the first action after the RCE.
- **Not available for:** design, reissue or provisional applications, PCT applications before national stage entry, or reexamination.
- **Annual cap:** **20,000** accepted requests per fiscal year, starting FY2025 (90 FR 29990, effective July 8, 2025). The cap was 15,000 before that.
- **Form:** PTO/AIA/424. The USPTO says Track One gives "a final disposition within about twelve months" (Track One page).
- **Accelerated Examination was discontinued** for utility applications. No petitions are accepted on or after July 10, 2025 (90 FR 24324).

## Changes and open questions

- **Jan 19, 2025** (FY2025 rule): new continuing-application fees (1.17(w)) and new IDS size fees (1.17(v)). The rule also raised most fees. RCE, design and other fees went up as well.
- **July 8, 2025:** Track One cap raised from 15,000 to 20,000 per fiscal year.
- **July 10, 2025:** Accelerated Examination ended for utility applications.
- **Aug 14, 2025:** expedited examination of design applications ("rocket docket") ended (90 FR 39124). Design is outside this skill.
- **Aug 13, 2026:** the 1.17(m)(1) higher-fee line moved from 2 years to 1 year.
- **Nov 1, 2026:** one foreign-office PCT search fee changed. Nothing in the utility filing path changed.
- *(unconfirmed)* Status of the USPTO fee-setting authority after about Dec 11 or 12, 2026.
- *(unconfirmed)* A 2025 press-reported idea of a value-based patent fee. No Federal Register proposal was found.
